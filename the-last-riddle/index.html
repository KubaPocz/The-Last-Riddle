The Last Riddle

The Last Riddle is a 3D puzzle game created in Unity, marking my first project in game development. You take on the role of a royal spy undergoing a test of your skills. To prove your worth, you must solve a few puzzles, relying on logic, observation, and problem-solving abilities. Throughout the game, you explore mysterious underground chambers, searching for a way to escape. There are no traps or dangers—only your intellect will determine your success. Can you solve the final riddle and find your way to freedom?
